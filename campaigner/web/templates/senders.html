How these numbers work

  • Delivered = people emailed minus hard bounces. Mailbox providers don't tell senders about inbox vs spam placement, so nothing here can measure that.
  • Open rate is an estimate. Apple Mail preloads images, which inflates it. Outlook blocks images by default, which deflates it. Hits within 60s of sending, or from known scanners, are excluded.
  • Clicks are more reliable. Security scanners (Safe Links, Mimecast) are filtered the same way. A click also counts as an open.
  • Rates are per unique person, over sends that had tracking on.
