Send email / message

The email is saved in your Outlook Drafts folder, on top of your last email to them when there is one. Save draft leaves it there for you to finish; Send now sends it straight away. Either way it's added to their history.

Opens WhatsApp with this message ready; you press send there. Then Log as sent so it's in their history. No phone number with a country code is saved for this lead: add one on their page.

Copies the message and opens their profile; paste and send it there. Then Log as sent so it's in their history.
