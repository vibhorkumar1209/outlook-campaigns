LinkedIn engagement

For leads whose invite or first email has gone out, the queue gets a Like + comment task for one of their recent posts. It stops when they reply on any channel, and campaigns can turn it off in their Settings tab.

LinkedIn detection

Accepts and replies are read from LinkedIn's notification emails (choose the inbox on Senders). In LinkedIn → Settings → Communications → Email, keep these on:

  • Invitations: invitation accepted
  • Messages: new messages

Detection matches the lead's full name in the notification. If it misses one, use Mark connected or They replied.
